# tmux send-keys as a content transport

Tempting for instant delivery; declined for content because
injected text arrives as a user-role turn, impersonating the owner in
the recipient's transcript (WAKE_WEARS_AUTHORITY), and because it is
unreviewable and unretractable the instant it is sent
(MUTABLE_UNTIL_READ). Two independent grounds converging on one rule
is WAKE_PHRASE. The sanctioned residue: an owner-triggered keybinding
that types the fixed wake phrase — the human is the authority, and
the phrase carries nothing.
