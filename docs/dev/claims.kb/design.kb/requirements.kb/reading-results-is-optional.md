# Dispatching many jobs costs a constant number of acts, and reading is optional

Dispatching N jobs costs the delegator a constant number of acts.
Reading any individual result is optional: the delegator can know what
to do next from an aggregate verdict alone.

A demonstration at ten or more jobs suffices; that number is the
agent's choice of a convincing size, not the person's.
