# Interrupting a sub-agent also interrupts its orchestrator

When the person interrupts a sub-agent to redirect or clarify it, the
orchestrator is interrupted too, with an agent-stopped-by-user
notification. The person's way of controlling the sub-agent and the
orchestrator's are one path.
