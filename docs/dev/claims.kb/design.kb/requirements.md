# Requirements

**How do we validate that the goals are achieved?**

Each claim in `requirements.kb/` is a test somebody could actually
write, stated so that failing it is observable. Where one cannot be, it
does not belong here.

A **probe** is a job instructed to overstep, run to prove enforcement:
a probe that reads outside its reach and is denied proves confinement
in a way inspecting configuration never does. Several requirements are
written in that form deliberately.

Self-report is never evidence. Where a requirement says "the record
shows", it means a record written by something other than the delegate.

Most requirements here are agent-signed: they are the tests the
agent-authored acceptance criteria proposed for goals the person has
since ruled on, and each stands until vetoed. The user-signed ones
restate a ruling in the person's own words.
