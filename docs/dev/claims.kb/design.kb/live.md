# A live channel wakes; the postbox carries

A live channel — anything that reaches a session in real time — may
exist only to wake: to say that mail waits, never what it says. The
claims here are the two grounds: whatever a live channel carries is
unretractable the instant it is sent, and what some channels inject
arrives in the owner's own voice.
