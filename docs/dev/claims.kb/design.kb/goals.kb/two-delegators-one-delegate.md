# The person and the orchestrator are two delegators of one delegate

The person and the orchestrating agent are both delegators of the same
delegate, each with a conversation channel and a control channel of
their own. A control from one, an interrupt or a message, neither stops
nor notifies the other. One delegator's view of the delegate's activity
is not another's.

Prevents: STOP_PROPAGATES and FORK_LEAKS, the two lived failures that
are one channel where there should be two.

Declined: treating the person as reaching the delegate only through the
orchestrator, which is the fused channel the lived failures describe.
