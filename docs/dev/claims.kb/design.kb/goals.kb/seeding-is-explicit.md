# A delegate begins from an explicitly chosen, recorded basis

A delegate begins from an explicitly chosen basis: nothing, a named
brief, or a designated slice of the delegator's context. Which one, and
exactly what was transmitted, is recorded.

Prevents: unintended inheritance, where the delegate absorbs the
delegator's assumptions through a channel nobody declared, so its
agreement means nothing. The fourth reason to delegate (REASONS), a
reading free of the delegator's assumptions, is unfalsifiable without
this.
