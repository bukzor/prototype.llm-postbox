# One mechanism serves every pairing of grades

One mechanism serves every pairing of delegator and delegate grade,
downward and upward alike (RANK). What differs by rank, the delegate's
reach and what the delegator can check, is declared per job.

- how much reach the delegate needs (CONFINE), which may exceed what a
  lower-grade delegator would grant a peer;
- what the delegator can check about the result (EVIDENCE): its
  correctness, or only its form against the spec.

Declined: a second mechanism for the upward case.
