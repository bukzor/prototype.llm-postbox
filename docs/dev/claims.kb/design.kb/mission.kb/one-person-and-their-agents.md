# One person and the agents orchestrating for them

The beneficiary is one person working through claude-code, together
with the agents orchestrating on their behalf. The person and an
orchestrating agent are both delegators, often of the same delegate at
the same time, and each holds authority over it in their own right.

Several people sharing one mechanism is out of scope until it happens.
The tripwire that says it has happened is ONE_OWNER.
