# How delegations compose is out of scope

The mechanism dispatches, confines, observes, converses with, and judges
one delegate at a time. How many delegations are arranged, as a fan-out,
a pipeline, a graph, or a nest, is not its concern; whatever arranges
them is a separate layer.

Declined: the earlier draft fixed fan-out-with-a-gate as the one shape
that matters and deferred a second shape until needed. Neither the
restriction nor the deferral pertains to the mechanism. Revisited only
if a use in USES turns out to need the mechanism itself to arrange
delegations.
