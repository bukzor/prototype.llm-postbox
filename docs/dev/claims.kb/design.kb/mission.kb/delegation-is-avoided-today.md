# Today the person avoids delegating

The person mostly avoids delegating in claude-code, and refuses the
built-in agent command outright. Two reasons, in their words: it goes
wrong often, and a delegate lacks what a session has: direct
connection, history rollback, and more.

The evidence is the lived failures in `experience.kb/`; six of them are
exactly session capabilities a delegate lacks (DEGRADED). A review of
their usage nonetheless recommended sparing use of a higher-grade agent
as a delegate rather than at the top level, which is what makes the
avoidance a problem worth solving rather than a preference to respect.
